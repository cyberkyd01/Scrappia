Scrappia quick guide

Get product names and prices from an Amazon search, page after page, into an Excel file. About 10 minutes. No technical knowledge needed.
How this works: Scrappia opens two windows. The Scrappia window has the buttons. The browser window shows Amazon. Whenever a button is highlighted in red in a picture below, that is the thing to click.
  • When a Pick button is on, clicking in the browser window captures what you click instead of opening it.
  • Press Esc in the browser window (or click Off) to browse normally again.
1

Start a new recipe

Open Scrappia. On the Home screen click New recipe.

2

Paste the Amazon search address

In your normal browser, search Amazon for what you want (for example "wireless headphones"). Copy the address from the top of the browser. Paste it into the box and click Open recorder.

A second window opens with Amazon inside it. Wait until the products are visible. If Amazon asks you to type some characters, do that in the second window first.
3

Switch on "Pick list"

In the Scrappia window click Pick list at the top. From now on, clicks inside the Amazon window are captured.

4

Click one product name in the Amazon window

Click the name of any product in the results. Scrappia looks at that product, finds all the others that look the same, and lists what it can read from them.

If the wrong thing gets highlighted (for example the whole page), click Off, then Pick list again and click a different part of a product, such as its price.
5

Keep only name and price, then use the list

The panel on the right now says how many products were found (here: 16 records) and shows everything Scrappia can read from them. Tick title and price, untick the rest, and click the blue button Use this list with 2 field(s) at the bottom of the panel.

6

Check the preview

The bottom of the Scrappia window now shows one row per product with its name and price. The left side shows your recipe: a list with two fields.

A few rows may have no price. That is normal: Amazon shows some results (sponsored items, bundles) in a different layout.
7

Switch to "Pick field" and click the Next button

Click Pick field in the Scrappia window. Then scroll to the bottom of the Amazon window and click the Next button of the page numbers.

8

Tell Scrappia this is the "next page" button

In the panel on the right click Set as next page. Scrappia will now move through the pages on its own.

If a site has a "Load more" button instead of page numbers, click that button in step 7 and choose Set as load more here instead. For pages that load more products as you scroll, choose Infinite scroll.
9

Choose how many pages (optional)

In the recipe on the left, click Pagination. In Max pages type how many pages you want, for example 2. Leave it empty or -1 for all pages.

10

Try it: "Test run (5 pages)"

Click Test run (5 pages) at the bottom. Rows arrive while it runs. When the status says succeeded you should see products from more than one page. In this example: 2 pages · 32 rows · 0 failed.

If it says 0 rows, read the grey log lines under the status: they say what happened (for example "the site refused the request"). Scrappia switches to its browser automatically when Amazon refuses a plain request.
11

Give it a name and save

Type a name in the name box at the top, then click Save.

12

Run it for real

Click Run. The Run monitor shows the progress. When it says succeeded, open the Exports tab and click Open file to open the Excel file. (If you did not choose an output, Scrappia writes an Excel file for you.) In this example the run collected 32 rows from 2 pages.

Next time

Your recipe is on the Home screen. Click Run next to it whenever you want fresh data, or open it and add a Schedule (gear button, Schedule tab) to run it every day. Excel is the default export; the gear button's Outputs tab adds CSV, Google Sheets, a database and more.

If something goes wrong

Amazon shows a puzzle ("type the characters"): solve it in the Amazon window; the run continues by itself.
Only one page was scraped: click Pagination on the left and check the selector. It should be something short like a.s-pagination-next, not a long text containing "page 2". Replace it if needed and test again.
Prices look wrong: click the price field on the left, and under Transforms add currency. It turns "$29.99" into the number 29.99.
Want to type your own search words? Gear button, General tab: add a keyword list and put {{keyword}} in the address.
